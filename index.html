About CyberSphere

A student-led initiative for digital safety at New Millennium School

Akshara Dhakan

Creator & Student Lead, New Millennium School

🛡 CyberSphere Founder 📚 New Millennium School 💡 Digital Safety Advocate

I built CyberSphere because I noticed students around me didn't have a place to learn about online safety in a way that actually made sense or felt safe to ask for help. I wanted to change that. This site is my answer: a student-built space where we look out for each other online.

OUR MISSION
To make every student at New Millennium School digitally aware, personally empowered, and genuinely safe online not through lectures, but through experience.
The internet is the most powerful tool our generation will ever use. But with that power comes real risk cyberbullying, phishing, misinformation, identity theft, and more. Most schools teach digital safety once a year in a PowerPoint. We believe that's not enough. CyberSphere exists to make safety a daily habit, not a one-off lesson. Every game you play, every tip you read, every report you submit it all builds a school community that knows how to protect itself.

🎯 THE PROBLEM WE'RE SOLVING

Every day, young people face threats online that they weren't taught to recognise. Studies show that 1 in 3 students has experienced some form of cyberbullying. Phishing attacks targeting young people have increased dramatically. Many students feel they have nowhere to report issues confidentially at school.

Standard digital literacy education is often too generic, too brief, and too forgettable. Students need to experience spotting a phishing email not just hear about it.

💡 OUR APPROACH

CyberSphere is built on the belief that learning by doing is the only way safety knowledge sticks. Instead of telling you what phishing looks like, we show you five realistic emails and make you decide then explain why you got it right or wrong.

Instead of a helpline number on a poster, we give you a real confidential reporting system with reference codes and admin replies. Instead of a one-time assembly, we give you a daily challenge that keeps safety top of mind.

Our Three Pillars

🧠

EDUCATE

We go beyond awareness posters. Interactive games, timed challenges, and real-world scenario simulations put you in the driver's seat. You don't just learn what a phishing email is you learn to spot one in three seconds.

🤝

SUPPORT

No student should feel alone when something goes wrong online. Our confidential reporting system means you can share what happened, receive a personal response, and get the help you need without fear of judgement or exposure.

🌐

COMMUNITY

Digital safety isn't a solo mission. The gallery, suggestions box, leaderboard, and weekly spotlight turn individual learning into a shared school culture. When we look out for each other, everyone is safer.

📅 HOW CYBERSPHERE CAME TO BE

THE SPARK

A classmate told me they'd been receiving threatening messages online and didn't know who to tell or what to do. They were scared. I realised our school had no proper channel for this and I wanted to build one.

THE RESEARCH

I spent time researching how students learn best, what makes cyber safety training stick, and what real threats young people face. I looked at phishing techniques, AI-generated content, password attacks, and online grooming tactics so I could teach others to recognise them.

THE BUILD

CyberSphere was built entirely by me from the dark neon design to the phishing game scenarios, the admin PIN system, the Real or AI? rounds, and every line of code. My goal was a site that looked serious, felt exciting, and actually taught something.

THE MISSION CONTINUES

CyberSphere isn't finished it's a living platform. Every suggestion submitted, every report filed, every leaderboard score, and every admin spotlight helps shape what comes next. This is our school's safety hub, and it belongs to all of us.
